### Pattern B: New `real_cte_at` companions (operations with cte_at but no real_cte_at)

All operations with `valid_cte_at_typ` or `xxx_typ_at`-based `cte_at` proofs have been given `real_cte_at` companions. The key technique is:
- Operations with `xxx_typ_at[wp]` in the wp set: `by (wp real_cte_at_typ_valid)`
- Operations using `valid_cte_at_typ`: `by (rule real_cte_at_typ_valid [OF xxx_typ_at])`
- Locale-scoped crunch rules: `lemmas (in Locale) name[wp] = real_cte_at_typ_valid [OF crunch_typ_at]`

## Downstream impact note

All six strengthened lemmas give postconditions that are **strictly stronger** than
before. Since `real_cte_at ⟹ cte_at`, any downstream proof in Refine or InfoFlow
that uses these lemmas via `[wp]` and expected `cte_at` will now receive `real_cte_at`
instead — which is fine (monotonicity). No downstream files expected to break.

The `lookup_slot_cte_at_wp` and `lookup_slot_real_cte_at_wp` are now
**duplicate lemmas** with identical statements in CSpace_AI.thy. Both names remain for
downstream compat (callers in Refine/InfoFlow use `lookup_slot_cte_at_wp` by name).
