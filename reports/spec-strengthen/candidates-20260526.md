## Pattern A — paired weak/strong (CAUTION: 70-80% false-positive rate)

**Warning**: Pattern A from scanner is unreliable. Two failure modes:
  1. **Wrong direction** — the "weak" lemma is the granular building block, the "strong" is a compound. Deleting building blocks breaks the compound proof.
  2. **Cross-op false positives** — two lemmas may share a body identifier (e.g. both call `set_cap`) but have incomparable preconditions; they aren't substitutable.

**Always read both lemmas + grep cross-file usages before acting.** Even when a real Pattern A pair exists, deletion of the weak form rarely shortens downstream proofs — consumers receive the equivalent stronger postcondition which they may need to weaken back.
