`lsfco_cte_at` is explicit-`rule`/`wp` use (not implicit `[wp]`), so grep is a

**Verdict: in-place statement change is VETOED** — strengthening the postcondition
breaks every wp-chain/`rule` consumer expecting `cte_at`, across three sessions and
five architectures. This is exactly the "expensive, cross-session" class the
protocol says to avoid, and it vindicates both the additive retreat in 0054 and the
user's cost concern with data.

## Step 2 — demand check (is the strength even needed?)

The same-file consumer `lsfco_cte_wp_at_univ` (Ipc_AI:399) does
`rule lsfco_cte_at` → `clarsimp simp: cte_wp_at_def`: it needs only `cte_at`. The
cross-session sites all consume `lsfco_cte_at` for `cte_at` in wp-chains.
**No current consumer needs `real_cte_at`.** ⇒ 0054's `lsfco_real_cte_at` is a
**speculative additive with zero demand**, not just zero realized consumers.

## Step 3 — the only safe, contained action

Since (a) in-place is vetoed and (b) there is no demand, the genuine-value
strengthening is not available. The one safe, statement-preserving "contract" step:
make the weak lemma a corollary of the strong one (DRY), giving the orphan
`lsfco_real_cte_at` its first in-source consumer — **statement unchanged ⇒ zero
cross-session blast radius**:

```isabelle
lemma lsfco_real_cte_at:                 -- moved above, now canonical
  "⦃valid_objs and valid_cap cn⦄ lookup_slot_for_cnode_op f cn idx depth ⦃λrv. real_cte_at rv⦄,-"
  by (rule lookup_cnode_slot_real_cte)

lemma lsfco_cte_at:                       -- statement IDENTICAL; now derives from the strong lemma
  "⦃valid_objs and valid_cap cn⦄ lookup_slot_for_cnode_op f cn idx depth ⦃λrv. cte_at rv⦄,-"
  by (rule hoare_strengthen_postE_R, rule lsfco_real_cte_at, simp add: real_cte_at_cte)
```
Patch: `logs/0029redo-lsfco-contract.patch`. Honest framing: this is a
**mechanism + metric demo** (0→1 realized consumer for the speculative lemma), NOT a
deep value win — no downstream guarantee gains `real_cte_at`.
